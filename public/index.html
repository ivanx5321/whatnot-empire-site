It depends on what you need, and I go over it on the call. Most sellers invest in coaching plus about $5K in inventory to start.

Do I need to be selling already?

No. Most of my students start from zero. You do need to be ready to invest in inventory and go live consistently.

TikTok Live or Whatnot?

Both are in the program. TikTok Live has the volume, but approval is strict, so I walk you through it. Whatnot is easier to start and pays out instantly. Most students run both.

Do I need a warehouse?

No. I started in a 150 sq ft storage unit with two pallets.

Can I do this with a full-time job?

Yes. Run evening shows to start, then hire a host once it pays. I'll show you how I hire and train mine.

Yes. My shows are run by hired hosts. What sells is the inventory, the pricing and free shipping, not your personality.
